Where is the cat? (Prepositions) 🐈

💡 Grammar: We use these words to describe where things are!
English Portuguese Listen
The cat is IN the box. O gato está DENTRO da caixa.
The cat is ON the table. O gato está EM CIMA da mesa.
The cat is UNDER the bed. O gato está EMBAIXO da cama.
The cat is BEHIND the sofa. O gato está ATRÁS do sofá.

Putting it all together! 🧩

Sentence Listen
My mom is in the kitchen.
The TV is on the table.
My shoes are under the bed.
I am sleeping in my bedroom.
