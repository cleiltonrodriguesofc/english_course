Demonstrative Pronouns ✨

👉 🍎

Learn how to point at things that are near and far!

Things that are NEAR 📍 (Perto)

👇
THIS
Este / Esta / Isso
(Singular - 1 thing)
👐
THESE
Estes / Estas
(Plural - 2+ things)

Ex: This is my book. (Este é meu livro)

Ex: These are my friends. (Estes são meus amigos)

Things that are FAR 🔭 (Longe)

👉
THAT
Aquele / Aquela / Aquilo
(Singular - 1 thing)
🌌
THOSE
Aqueles / Aquelas
(Plural - 2+ things)

Ex: That is a bird. (Aquele é um pássaro)

Ex: Those are stars. (Aquelas são estrelas)
